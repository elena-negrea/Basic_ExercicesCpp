#EUCLID -substraction method
- the greatest common divisor of two numbers also divides their difference. The algorithm works as follows:
    +   While the two numbers are different, subtract the smaller number from the larger one.
    +   When the two numbers become equal, their common value is the greatest common divisor of the original numbers.
    +   The algorithm cannot be applied if one of the numbers is 0.

#EUCLID -division method

GCD of any two numbers is never negative or 0, and the least positive integer common to any two numbers is always 1.

    To find the GCD:
- by listing factors of each number, find the one they share, and pick the biggest one
- by Euclidean Algorithm: Divide the larger number to the smaller, then take the divisor and divide it by the remainder. Repeat this process until the remainder is 0
    The final non-zero remainder is your GCD
